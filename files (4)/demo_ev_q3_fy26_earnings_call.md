## Opening Remarks

Good evening and welcome to the Demo EV Motors third quarter fiscal 2026 earnings call. This quarter we delivered our strongest operating performance since listing, driven by a sharp increase in electric vehicle volumes and a disciplined approach to costs and the balance sheet.

## Financial and Operational Highlights
[[PAGE 2]]
Consolidated revenue grew 16.8% year on year to Rs 4,210 crore in the third quarter, ahead of the 12% growth we had guided to at the start of the year. Passenger vehicle volumes rose 19% while commercial vehicle volumes were broadly flat.

EBITDA margins for the EV segment expanded by 180 bps year on year to 8.4%, driven by localized battery pack sourcing and higher production scale at our main plant. Consolidated EBITDA margin improved to 12.1% from 11.0% in the same quarter last year.

Profit after tax was Rs 312 crore compared with Rs 241 crore a year ago, reflecting both higher volumes and lower finance costs.

[[PAGE 3]]
## Balance Sheet and Cash Flow
Net debt was reduced to Rs 1,900 crore, down 11% quarter on quarter, after we prepaid two term loans using internal accruals. Net debt to EBITDA improved to 1.4 times from 1.9 times a year ago.

Cash flow from operations was Rs 640 crore, well above reported profit after tax, supported by tighter working capital management and faster dealer collections. Management remains committed to a further reduction in gross borrowings over the next four quarters.

## Capital Expenditure Plan
We continue to expect capital expenditure of around Rs 900 crore for the full year, with the majority directed to battery pack assembly capacity and a new charging software platform. We do not plan to raise additional debt to fund this programme.

[[PAGE 4]]
## Outlook and Guidance
For the fourth quarter we expect revenue growth in the mid-teens and EV segment EBITDA margins to be sustained at or above current levels. Full year consolidated EBITDA margin guidance is raised to between 11.8% and 12.2%.

## Risks and Regulatory Matters
Management sees three main risks. First, battery cell prices remain volatile and a sustained rise could pressure margins. Second, any reduction in government purchase incentives for electric vehicles would affect demand in price sensitive segments. Third, a pending regulatory review of one component supplier is ongoing; the company does not expect a material financial impact but will disclose any developments as required under the listing regulations.
